Buttons, badges, dialogs, menus, dropdowns, tabs, and form fields now use the shared Omni components, so they match the rest of Omni: dialogs keep a gutter on phones and scroll when tall, and confirmations name what they act on.

Press `?` anywhere, or pick "Keyboard shortcuts" in the command palette, to see every keyboard shortcut, including the workflow editor's. Press `b` to collapse or expand the sidebar.
